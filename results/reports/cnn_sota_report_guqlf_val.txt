NOTES AND CAVEATS
  * SPLIT: everything here is VAL. No TEST number exists for this line yet.
  * BINNING: the official SNR axis is degenerate on this line (all truth DLAs
    SNR >= 16.7). T2/T3/T4 are the informative tables. They re-use the scorer's
    exact per-bin formulas; T1 is the self-check that reproduces
    score_catalog's detection_score bit-for-bit.
  * DECODE CHOICE: headline uses count_bias=[0,0,0] so both systems are compared
    under an identical, untuned decode. The b-sweep was selected on this same
    val split; its gain over the gate decode is +0.02pp here, i.e. within
    selection noise. Quote the gate number for comparisons.
  * Single seed per member. Per project noise discipline the CNN tower path
    (train_hybrid.py) is NOT bitwise reproducible (+-2..9pp per epoch), so with
    one seed effects below ~2pp are not separable from noise.
  * n_pred / purity are computed on val only; the low-SNR tail of val is not
    identical to the TEST tail. On this line val has NO low-SNR tail at all.
  * Reading the bin tables: per-bin `Match` = min(n_match_truth_bin,
    n_match_pred_bin). Each side is binned by its OWN axis value, so a matched
    pair whose truth bin differs from its pred bin is not counted inside any
    single bin. Hence sum(bin Match) < overall n_match.
    Det is the weighted-F1-over-bins figure; weights = n_truth.
  * Bins with n_truth = 0 are omitted (scorer rule).
  * The fusion head is trained with both towers FROZEN; ep0 of a fusion arm is
    bitwise identical to the base tower's last epoch (residual zero-init).
  * Generated from cached five-head tensors => the decode and scoring steps are
    deterministic (no seed noise at this stage).
